= ONTAP post-upgrade verifications workflow
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
After you upgrade ONTAP, there are several tasks you should perform to verify your cluster readiness.

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-1.png["One"] Verify your cluster.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
After you upgrade ONTAP, you should link:task_verify_cluster_after_upgrade.html[verify your cluster version, cluster health, and storage health]. If you are using a MetroCluster FC configuration, you also need to verify that the cluster is enabled for automatic unplanned switchover.  

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-2.png["Two"] Verify that all LIFs are on home ports.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
During a reboot, some LIFs might have been migrated to their assigned failover ports. After you upgrade a cluster, you must link:task_enabling_and_reverting_lifs_to_home_ports_post_upgrade.html[enable and revert any LIFs that are not on their home ports].

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-3.png["Three"] Verify special considerations specific to your cluster.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
If certain configurations exist on your cluster, you might need to link:concept_special_configurations_post_checks.html[perform additional steps after you upgrade].

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-4.png["Four"] Update the Disk Qualification Package (DQP).

[role="quick-margin-para"]
The DQP is not updated as part of an ONTAP upgrade.  You must link:concept_when_you_need_to_update_the_disk_qualification_package.html[update the DQP] after the upgrade.
